Auditor-side controls: wrong-context-summary fusion and summary-only score both fall back to baseline level (24/74 each) — the gain requires current-state-carried information.

**Accepted positive knowledge (at PROOF OF CONCEPT ceiling, binding wording in VALIDATED_MECHANISMS).**
1. In SPIDER's Graph setting, stepwise fused task+current-state-summary retrieval (top-M=max(3k,20), greedy MMR λ=0.7) addresses executable-correct fragments strictly more often than task-text-only retrieval with identical reranking machinery under paraphrased goals and shifted entry contexts.
2. The advantage requires the correct current-state summary (wrong-context or summary-only controls fall back to baseline).
3. Under identical policies the retrieval advantage converted into fewer novel actions than task-only retrieval on both goal regimes, at equal-or-higher subgoal success.

**Accepted negative/cautionary results (preserved so they are not rediscovered).**
1. Relative to hand-authored exact `goal_sig` addressing, the mechanism wins ONLY in the natural-language-consumer regime; when signatures exist, exact addressing remains more action-efficient (21 vs 39 novel on the literal slice).
2. No advantage over task-only retrieval for login/pager addressing; wins concentrate in one site's dynamic-content menu-disambiguation cluster.
3. alpha=0.4 vs alpha=0.5 (paper/code discrepancy): identical rankings everywhere at this library scale — not worth ablating again at similar scale.
4. MMR lifts @3 recall equally for B and C (68/74); the @1 gain is attributable to the state term.
5. Mechanically-derived descriptions are weak keys for some clusters (books travel/open-first/paginate unsolved at hard@1 for ALL text conditions): description quality, not fusion, is the binding constraint there.
6. Constant-text fusion controls are unstable under the lexical-hash embedder (sha1 bucket collisions at d=512) and are never citable as evidence.
7. WebArena headline numbers were NOT reproduced (out of prereg scope); same-site reuse only — cross-site transfer untested by SPIDER AND by SGDR's authors.
