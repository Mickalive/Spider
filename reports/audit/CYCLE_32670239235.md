### Findings against TEAM PHYSICS

- **F-P1 (evidence ephemerality — limits reuse strength).** The corpus
  `wp003b_v2_transitions.jsonl` and raw DOM snapshots lived only in `/tmp`
  (disclosed per §31) and are already gone. The digest chain
  (`input_sha256` in every result JSON = manifest sha256 =
  corpus_verification record) is self-consistent but **independently
  unverifiable**; the "0 mismatches" corpus verification is self-reported.
  All arithmetic above is internal-consistency recomputation, not raw-data
  replication. Required fix: commit compact row-level sufficient statistics
  (per-row feature vectors + labels + predictions) or the corpus itself next
  time.
- **F-P2 (post-freeze exclusion rewrite, disclosed).** The frozen loader's
  exclusion rule (`labs[0].get("ok")`) was un-runnable against real rows
  (labels are plain strings; per-action ok flags were never persisted). D2
  re-operationalized it post-freeze as "single-action chain whose only action
  failed". Mechanical, uniformly applied, disclosed — but the exact frozen
  rule never executed, so a residual cannot-be-excluded risk remains that the
  operationalization interacted with outcomes. Mitigated by exclusion being
  defined on pre-outcome action success and by the primary verdict being
  INCONCLUSIVE rather than decisional.
- **F-P3 (gate key construction deviates from its own definition — minor).**
  `gate()` caches `url_shape` once per `trajectory_id` instead of reading
  each visit's own snapshot, so keys merge states across steps of one
  trajectory. This can only inflate visits-per-key, i.e. inflate G1/G2;
  the gate fails by a wide margin (29 vs 50), so the DATA_INSUFFICIENT
  conclusion is robust. Fix before any future gate run.
- **F-P4 (unverifiable supporting detail).** "Independent out-of-pipeline
  reproduction M=0.8411" has no committed script/artifact. UNVERIFIED;
  non-load-bearing for any verdict.
- **F-P5 (latent inconsistency, harmless here).** Bootstrap averages d_s over
  ALL folds while the verdict counts adequate folds only; identical here
  because all 6 folds are adequate. Align them before corpora with weak folds.
- **F-P6 (test hygiene).** The seed-determinism unit test imports the whole
  collector module (hence playwright) just to call a sha256 helper; it errors
  in playwright-free environments. Mechanism itself verified stable manually.

### Physics bottom line
