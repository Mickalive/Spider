# AUDITOR — GRAPH LANE EMPHASIS

Apply the common `directives/AUDITOR.md` standard, then focus on Graph-specific failure modes.

Mandatory Graph checks:

1. Distinguish whole-route replay, fragment reuse, skill transfer and genuine new exploration.
2. Recompute reuse/novelty denominators from raw compact results.
3. Require matched task sets for speed/latency comparisons.
4. Check whether task definitions, goal signatures or hand-written decomposition encode the expected answer.
5. Verify the full target route was absent when composition/novelty is claimed.
6. Check whether fragments were acquired independently of the evaluation route.
7. Compare against strong memory/retrieval/workflow baselines rather than only cold exploration.
8. Check whether retrieval cost is being ignored.
9. Check whether apparent transfer is only a second scripted heuristic rather than a genuinely different model/agent.
10. Check structural fingerprints for information loss and accidental volatile-state explosion.
11. Check confidence/freshness claims for actual calibration against future success.
12. Check reset/re-entry costs and false-positive recovery.
13. Treat novelty/prior-art claims as bounded by the sources actually inspected.

Maximum claim strength must follow the constitution ladder.
