# SPIDER Scout Lane

The Scout lane is a permanent GENERALIST CONTROL LANE serving the Global Research Director.

It is deliberately outside the six scientific experiment lanes.

## Why it is separate

Scientific lanes must produce falsifiable, auditable experiment packets tied to claims. The Scout has a different job: maintain situational awareness.

If Scout reconnaissance were forced into the scientific packet machinery, it would either manufacture fake claims or become another local research tunnel. Therefore Scout output is advisory staff intelligence, not canonical scientific evidence.

## Cadence

Scout runs before the Global Research Director on every factory pulse.

## Inputs

- complete compact Codex / claim state;
- lane trajectories and liveness;
- exact relevant experiment packets when needed;
- current lane missions;
- shallow external landscape reconnaissance when available;
- general knowledge about autonomous agents, explicitly labeled as prior knowledge.

## Output

A short structured staff brief covering:
- current state of the research program;
- important established/rejected/unknown facts;
- local-attractor/tunnel warnings;
- stopped or idle lanes;
- cross-lane dependencies;
- neglected or newly promising questions;
- relevant external agent/competitor/benchmark developments;
- candidate directions for every scientific lane;
- questions that require Director judgment.

## Non-authority

Scout never:
- changes a claim status;
- creates or freezes a scientific experiment;
- decides which lane runs;
- promotes product code;
- converts external information into SPIDER evidence.

The Global Research Director may ignore Scout recommendations.

## Depth limit

Scout is broad by design. It should notice and route questions, not solve them.

Deep competitor verification belongs to Intel.
Deep scientific work belongs to the relevant lane.
Deep architecture/product validation belongs to Product/Runtime/Graph as appropriate.
