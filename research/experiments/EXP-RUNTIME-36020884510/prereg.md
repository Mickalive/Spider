## 10. Uncertainty method

- `n_non304` counts and `hs_rate` Wilson `95%` lower bound.
- `r` Fisher-z transformed CI `95%` with `n_non304` denominator; `CramersV` also; `p` from Fisher-z TOST equivalent for |r|<0.30 bound (correct tail `1-Phi`), not wrong tail.
- `rho_shuffled`: block permutation `B=1000` (block=`trajectory_id` integer sums), within-f `std` and CI width reported; `effective>1` required; `p` = proportion `|rho_perm| >= |rho_obs|` (two-sided) via trajectory-block permutations only.
