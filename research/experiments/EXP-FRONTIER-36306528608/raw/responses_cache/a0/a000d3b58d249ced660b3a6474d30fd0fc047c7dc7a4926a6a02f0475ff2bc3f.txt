<p id="mwEg">
<br id="mwEw"/>
<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia" title="Wikipedia" id="mwFA">Wikipedia</a> is a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Free_encyclopedia" title="Wikipedia:Free encyclopedia" id="mwFQ">free online encyclopedia</a> that <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Introduction_to_Wikipedia" title="Help:Introduction to Wikipedia" id="mwFg">anyone can edit</a>, and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Wikipedians#Number_of_editors" title="Wikipedia:Wikipedians" id="mwFw">millions already have</a>.</p>

<p id="mwGA"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Purpose" title="Wikipedia:Purpose" id="mwGQ">Wikipedia's purpose</a> is to present information on all branches of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge" title="Knowledge" id="mwGg">knowledge</a>. It consists of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Editing" title="Help:Editing" id="mwGw">freely editable</a> content, with articles that contain numerous links guiding readers to more information. Wikipedia is one of many <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Foundation#Projects_and_initiatives" title="Wikimedia Foundation" id="mwHA">Wikimedia projects</a> hosted by the nonprofit <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Wikimedia_Foundation" title="Wikipedia:Wikimedia Foundation" id="mwHQ">Wikimedia Foundation</a>.</p>

<p id="mwHg"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Wikipedia_is_written_by_humans,_for_humans" title="Wikipedia:Wikipedia is written by humans, for humans" id="mwHw">Wikipedia is written by human</a> volunteers known as <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia_community" title="Wikipedia community" id="mwIA">Wikipedians</a> who <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_writing" title="Collaborative writing" id="mwIQ">collaborate</a> with <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Editing_policy#Artificial_intelligence_additions" title="Wikipedia:Editing policy" id="mwIg">almost no assistance from artificial intelligence</a>. Wikipedia articles can be edited by <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Who_writes_Wikipedia%3F" title="Wikipedia:Who writes Wikipedia?" id="mwIw">anyone</a> with <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_access" title="Internet access" id="mwJA">Internet access</a>, except in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Protection_policy" title="Wikipedia:Protection policy" id="mwJQ">limited cases</a> in which editing is <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Protection_policy#Edit_protection" title="Wikipedia:Protection policy" id="mwJg">restricted</a> to prevent <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Disruption" title="Wikipedia:Disruption" class="mw-redirect" id="mwJw">disruption</a> or <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:VAND" title="Wikipedia:VAND" class="mw-redirect" id="mwKA">vandalism</a>. Since its creation on <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia_Day" title="Wikipedia Day" class="mw-redirect" id="mwKQ">January 15, 2001</a>, it has grown into the world's <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Size_of_Wikipedia" title="Wikipedia:Size of Wikipedia" id="mwKg">largest</a> reference <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Website" title="Website" id="mwKw">website</a>, attracting <a rel="mw:WikiLink/Interwiki" href="https://stats.wikimedia.org/#/all-wikipedia-projects/reading/unique-devices" class="extiw" id="mwLA">over a billion visitors each month</a>. Wikipedia is a multilingual project, with more than <a rel="mw:WikiLink/Interwiki" href="https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/List%20of%20Wikipedias#All_Wikipedias_ordered_by_number_of_articles" title="m:List of Wikipedias" class="extiw" id="mwLQ"><span about="#mwt13" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwLg" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"Number to word","href":"./Template:Number_to_word"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"{{rounddown|{{NUMBEROF|ARTICLES|totalactive}}|-6}}"}},"i":0}}]}'>sixty-eight million</span> articles</a> in more than <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special:SiteMatrix" title="Special:SiteMatrix" id="mwLw">300 separate wikis</a> for almost as many languages. This includes <span about="#mwt11" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwMA" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"NUMBEROFARTICLES","function":"numberofarticles"},"params":{},"i":0}}]}'>7,245,150</span> articles in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language" title="English language" id="mwMQ">English</a>, with <span about="#mwt12" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwMg" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"NUMBEROFACTIVEUSERS","function":"numberofactiveusers"},"params":{},"i":0}}]}'>267,685</span> active contributors in the past month.</p>

<p id="mwUw">Wikipedia's fundamental principles are summarized in its <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Five_pillars" title="Wikipedia:Five pillars" id="mwVA">five pillars</a>. While the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia_community" title="Wikipedia community" id="mwVQ">Wikipedia community</a> has developed many <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Policies_and_guidelines" title="Wikipedia:Policies and guidelines" id="mwVg">policies and guidelines</a>, new editors do not need to be familiar with them before they start contributing.</p>

<p id="mwVw">The quality of content is more important than the expertise of who contributes it. Wikipedia's content must conform with its <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Editing_policy" title="Wikipedia:Editing policy" id="mwWA">policies</a>, including being <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Verifiable" title="Wikipedia:Verifiable" class="mw-redirect" id="mwWQ">verifiable</a> by published <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources" title="Wikipedia:Reliable sources" id="mwWg">reliable sources</a>. Contributions based on <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Neutral_point_of_view" title="Wikipedia:Neutral point of view" id="mwWw">personal opinions</a>, beliefs, or personal experiences, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Original_research" title="Wikipedia:Original research" class="mw-redirect" id="mwXA">unreviewed research</a>, libellous material, and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyright_violations" title="Wikipedia:Copyright violations" id="mwXQ">copyright violations</a> are not allowed and will not remain. Wikipedia's software makes it easy to reverse errors, and experienced <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia_editor" title="Wikipedia editor" class="mw-redirect" id="mwXg">editors</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Watchlist" title="Wikipedia:Watchlist" class="mw-redirect" id="mwXw">watch</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Recent_changes_patrol" title="Wikipedia:Recent changes patrol" id="mwYA">patrol</a> bad edits.</p>

<p id="mwYQ"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:NOTPAPER" title="Wikipedia:NOTPAPER" class="mw-redirect" id="mwYg">Wikipedia differs from printed references in important ways</a>. Anyone can instantly improve it, add quality information, remove misinformation, and fix errors and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Vandalism" title="Wikipedia:Vandalism" id="mwYw">vandalism</a>. Since Wikipedia is continually updated, encyclopedic articles on major news events appear within minutes.</p>

<p id="mwZA">For over <span about="#mwt14" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwZQ" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"Age in years","href":"./Template:Age_in_years"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"2001-01-15"}},"i":0}}]}'>25</span> years, Wikipedians have volunteered their time and talents to create history's most comprehensive encyclopedia while providing references and other resources to researchers worldwide (see <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Researching_with_Wikipedia" title="Wikipedia:Researching with Wikipedia" id="mwZg">Researching with Wikipedia</a>). In summary, since 2001 Wikipedia has tested the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisdom_of_the_crowd" title="Wisdom of the crowd" id="mwZw">wisdom of the crowd</a> and has found that it succeeds.</p>
