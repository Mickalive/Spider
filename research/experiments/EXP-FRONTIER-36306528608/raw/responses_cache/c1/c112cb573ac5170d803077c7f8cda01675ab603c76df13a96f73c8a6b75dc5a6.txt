<table id="mwOg">
<tbody id="mwOw"><tr id="mwPA">
<td colspan="2" id="mwPQ">Please provide a clear, descriptive name by which your file will be known on Wikipedia.</td></tr>
<tr id="mwPg">
<td class="uwLegend" id="mwPw"><span class="uwObligatory" id="mwQA"><span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwQQ">&nbsp;</span></span></td>
<td style="" id="mwQg"><span id="placeholderInputName"></span><br id="mwQw"><small id="mwRA">This name must be unique across the whole of Wikipedia, so please make it informative and easy to recognize.<br id="mwRQ">It's no problem to use a fairly long name. It may also include spaces, commas and most other punctuation marks.<br id="mwRg">Please also note that file names are case sensitive (with the exception of the first letter).<br id="mwRw"><b id="mwSA">Good:</b> "City of London, skyline from London City Hall, Oct 2008.jpg". <b id="mwSQ">Bad</b>: "Skyline.jpg", "DSC0001234.jpg".</small></td></tr>
<tr class="mw-empty-elt" id="mwSg"></tr>

<div id="warningIllegalChars" class="uploadWarning" style="display:none;">
<p id="mwSw">Sorry, a few special characters and character combinations cannot be used in the filename for technical reasons. This goes especially for <b id="mwTA">#<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwTQ">&nbsp;</span>&lt;<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwTg">&nbsp;</span>&gt;<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwTw">&nbsp;</span>[<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwUA">&nbsp;</span>]<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwUQ">&nbsp;</span>|<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwUg">&nbsp;</span>:<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwUw">&nbsp;</span>{<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwVA">&nbsp;</span>}<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwVQ">&nbsp;</span>/<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwVg">&nbsp;</span></b> and <b id="mwVw"><span typeof="mw:Nowiki" id="mwWA">~~~</span></b>. Your filename has been modified to avoid these. Please check if it is okay now.</p>
</div>
<div id="warningBadFilename" class="uploadWarning" style="display:none;">
<p id="mwWQ">The filename you chose seems to be very short, or overly generic. Please don't use:</p>
<ul id="mwWg"><li id="mwWw">Titles that consist only of very generic descriptive words (e.g. "Sunset.jpg", "Townhall.jpg")</li>
<li id="mwXA">Titles that consist only of a person's first or last name, when that name is likely to be shared by many others (e.g. "John.jpg", "Miller.jpg")</li>
<li id="mwXQ">Titles that consist of mere numbers, of the kind often produced by digital cameras ("DSC_001234", "IMGP0345"), or random strings like those sometimes found on the web ("30996951316264l.jpg")</li></ul>
</div>

<div id="warningImageThumb">
