<p id="mwEg">The <b id="mwEw">1951 Miami Redskins football team</b> was an <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_football" title="American football" id="mwFA">American football</a> team that represented <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_University" title="Miami University" id="mwFQ">Miami University</a> in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-American_Conference" title="Mid-American Conference" id="mwFg">Mid-American Conference</a> (MAC) during the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951_college_football_season" title="1951 college football season" id="mwFw">1951 college football season</a>. In its first season under head coach <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ara_Parseghian" title="Ara Parseghian" id="mwGA">Ara Parseghian</a>, Miami compiled a 7–3 record (3–1 against MAC opponents), finished in second place in the MAC, and outscored all opponents by a combined total of 229 to 159.<sup about="#mwt22" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-1"}}'><a href="#cite_note-1" id="mwGQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwGg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGw">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHA">]</span></span></a></sup> The Redskins were ranked at No. 83 in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951_college_football_rankings#Litkenhous_Ratings" title="1951 college football rankings" id="mwHQ">1951 Litkenhous Ratings</a>.<sup about="#mwt25" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-2"}}'><a href="#cite_note-2" id="mwHg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwHw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwIA">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwIQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwIg">Donald Green was the team captain.<sup about="#mwt26" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-3"}}'><a href="#cite_note-3" id="mwIw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwJA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJQ">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJg">]</span></span></a></sup>  The team's statistical leaders included <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Pont" title="John Pont" id="mwJw">John Pont</a> with 883 rushing yards, Jim Root with 894 passing yards, and Clive Rush with 398 receiving yards.<sup about="#mwt27" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-4"}}'><a href="#cite_note-4" id="mwKA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwKQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKg">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
